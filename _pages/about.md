I am a PhD student in the Department of Economics at North Carolina State University. My research interests lie at the intersection of international trade, energy economics, and international finance, with a regional focus on West Africa.

Before starting my PhD, I worked in fintech, which shapes how I approach applied empirical questions in trade and finance today.

I am currently building out my research portfolio and am on the market for research assistant and pre-doctoral opportunities at policy-focused institutions.
